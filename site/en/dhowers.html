Dhowers

Dhowers brings together people who are serious about building things. We work on concrete problems in Physical AGI by running experiments, writing code, and building prototypes together.

Ways to work together

Ways to work together

Individuals
Bring a problem you want to solve, a prototype you’re working on, or a skill you’d like to contribute. We can have a conversation and find one small thing worth trying together.
Faculty and research teams
Design experiments around a shared question, and exchange methods and data. Where the project fits, we can coauthor a paper, release open-source work, or mentor students.
Companies and engineering teams
Start with a problem from a real setting. Together, we can define a measurable goal, build a prototype, run a field test, or assess technical feasibility.
