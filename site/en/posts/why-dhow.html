Why We Started Dhow

A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step. Something that matters may start as a vague idea. The direction gradually becomes clearer as we try things.

Dhow focuses on Physical AGI. By bringing software and hardware together, we want to connect digital AGI to the physical world so it can perceive its surroundings and act.

In embodied intelligence, we aim for zero-shot generalization to new tasks, dexterous manipulation, and timely responses to changes in the environment.

We hope to start conversations and do solid, worthwhile work together.
