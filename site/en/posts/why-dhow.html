Why We Started Dhow

A place to share what we’re working on and meet people who might want to work on it with us.

Some ideas start with a concrete problem. Working through it can lead to a piece of code, an experiment, a paper, or something other people can use. At Dhow, we want to share the process as well as the results, including what we tried, where we got stuck, and what we want to test next.

Dhow focuses on Physical AGI. We’ll start with questions we can test ourselves and document how we build and test robots and prototypes.

What you’ll find here

Research posts explain the problem and point to the code and results when they’re available. For projects, we’ll share things you can see, try, or reproduce. Notes can come out earlier, while our questions and judgments are still taking shape.

Why we call ourselves Dhowers

When you share a problem, you sometimes meet someone who’s thought about it from a different angle. One person might want to write code with you, another might help design a better experiment, and someone else might have just the right place to test it. We call these collaborators Dhowers.

If you have something concrete you’d like to work on, come talk to us on the Dhowers page.
